curseek

Hide-and-seek for cursors, in the dark.

How to play
  • One player is the seeker, the rest are hiders.
  • The maze is shown for 3 seconds, then fades to darkness. Memorize it.
  • Hiders start moving at once; the seeker waits until the lights are out.
  • You only see a small area around you. Walls block sight.
  • The seeker catches hiders by touching them. Survive 90 seconds to win.
  • Move with the mouse (click to capture it, Esc to release) or WASD / arrows.
